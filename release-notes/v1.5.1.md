# Cable limits on Apple chargers, a solid window, and fixes from your reports

## Charging

- **WhatCable now spots when the cable may be what's limiting an Apple charger** (#672, thanks @rcross). If a charger rated well above 60 W only offers 3 A and the cable's details couldn't be read, the card says "Cable may be limiting charging speed" and suggests a cable rated for 5 A, 100 W or more.
- **A charger that's plugged in but not being used now reads "Charger connected"**, not "Connect a charger". This happens when a charge-limit app holds your Mac on battery. It waits a few seconds before showing, so it doesn't flash as you plug in or unplug.

## Displays

- **No more false "Monitor can do more than it is set to" warning** when a monitor offers both 4K at 50 Hz and 4K at 60 Hz (#677, thanks @haxsmert). The best mode is now the one that draws the most pixels per second.

## App

- **New setting: a solid background for the menu bar window** (#562, suggested by @jimmyorz). The see-through background could get uncomfortably bright over light windows.
- **The window remembers its position and size** across launches (#522, thanks @PeterGi9313thub).
- **Accessibility tools that press the menu bar icon now open it** (#632, thanks @charlessnow).

## Pro terminal dashboard

- **Correct colours and no flicker in Terminal.app** (#579, thanks @official-Cromatin). The dashboard uses 256 colours unless your terminal says it supports full colour. To force full colour, run it with `COLORTERM=truecolor`.
- The dashboard is now translated.

## For scripts that read WhatCable's output

- `whatcable --json` and `--bench-report` pick the display's top mode the new way: the declared mode with the most picture pixels per second (width x height x refresh), not the highest pixel clock. So a CTA 4K at 50 Hz no longer outranks a reduced-blanking 4K at 60 Hz. `--bench-report` captures are now `schemaVersion` 4. `topDetailedTimingPixelClockHz`, `topDetailedTimingRefreshHz` and `topModeSource` keep their names and types.
- `whatcable --json` `charging.bottleneck` and `--bench-report` `diagnostics.charging.kind` have a new value, `possibleCableLimit`. It comes with two new integer fields: `chargerRatedW` (the rating in the charger's Apple name) and `offeredW` (its top offer now). Both are left out for every other value. With this value, `--bench-report`'s `negotiatedW` is the live contract and `chargerW` is the rating. The change only adds things, so `schemaVersion` stays 4, but code that switches on the value should handle it.
